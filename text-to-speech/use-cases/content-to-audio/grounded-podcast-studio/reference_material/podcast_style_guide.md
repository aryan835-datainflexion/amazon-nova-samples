# Polly Podcasts — Style Guide

This document is ingested into the Bedrock Knowledge Base and retrieved by the
script-writer agent. Edit it (and add more files to this folder) to shape the
voice of generated episodes, then re-run the ingestion job.

## Show identity

- Show name: **Polly Podcasts**.
- Format: a two-person conversation between a **host** and a **co-host**.
- Every episode opens with the host saying "Welcome to Polly Podcasts",
  introducing themselves, and introducing the co-host by name.

## Tone

- Warm, curious, and energetic — like two knowledgeable friends talking.
- Explain jargon in plain language the first time it appears.
- Keep sentences short enough to be spoken comfortably in one breath.
- Avoid filler ("um", "you know") and avoid reading statistics like a list;
  weave numbers into the conversation naturally.

## Structure of a turn

- Each turn is 2–4 sentences.
- Alternate strictly: host, co-host, host, co-host, ...
- The host generally frames questions and transitions; the co-host generally
  supplies detail and examples. Swap occasionally to keep it lively.
- Speakers should refer to each other by name at least once per episode.

## Accuracy

- Only state factual claims supported by the source-backed claims provided for
  the episode.
- If a fact is uncertain, phrase it as such ("early results suggest ...").
- Do not invent statistics, dates, names, or quotes.

## Do not include

- Stage directions, sound-effect cues, or bracketed annotations.
- Music references, ad reads, or timestamps.
- Markdown or formatting — output is spoken aloud.

## Sign-off

- Close warmly and thank the listener. Keep the final turn short.
